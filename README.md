# Andrea Brognera

Business student at H-FARM. I turn company data into systems people can trust.

I work in four steps: **track** the data, **qualify** it, **archive** it in a clear structure, **interpret** it.
AI does part of the work, and a human stays in the loop twice: as supervisor, and as the one who gives the feedback that keeps the system aligned over time.
Security comes in layers. Every project now starts with a governance part, and I build for the long term, not just for launch.

|---|---|
| **AGECO**, facility management platform (freelance) | Access rules for six roles, with row-level security. Automatic checks in CI enforce them. |
| **OPUS Training**, co-founder | The AI drafts training programs; the coach reviews and signs them before the athlete sees them. Checks in CI keep health data isolated and the vendor list in line with the privacy notice. |
